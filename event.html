Coding Competition

Hack-A-Thon

March 26, 2028 | 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Location: Tech Hub, Room 101

About the Event

Our Hack-A-Thon is designed to bring together developers of all skill levels to collaborate, innovate, and create amazing projects. Participants will have the opportunity to work in teams, brainstorm ideas, and develop solutions to real-world problems. The event will conclude with presentations of the projects and awards for the best solutions.

Event Schedule

  • 1:00 PM: Welcome & Introduction
  • 1:30 PM: Team Formation & Brainstorming
  • 2:00 PM: Coding Session
  • 3:30 PM: Project Presentations
  • 4:00 PM: Awards & Closing Remarks

What to Bring

  • Laptop and charger
  • Student ID
  • Notebook and pen

Accessibility & Participation

  • Wheelchair accessible venue
  • Quiet breakout spaces available
